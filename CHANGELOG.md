# Changelog

## 0.2 · 20 September 2026

Added four chapters: The Challenge, The Distance Between Claims, A History of the Question, and Counting Without Overclaiming. The full draft now has an opening essay and six chapters, approximately 14,600 words.

Expanded the bibliography to 71 scoped records and added 26 chapter-evidence records, with overlaps explicitly identified. The corpus baseline is unchanged. Reading order now follows the book map while preserving existing chapter URLs and legacy audio links. New synthetic narration accompanies the four additions. Independent human scholarly review and all proposed participant studies remain pending.

## 0.1 · 20 September 2026

First public research draft: opening essay, two sample chapters, phone listening edition, source catalog, descriptive corpus pilot, project explanation, and public contribution workflow.

The manuscript's research date is 16 September 2026. Public-release wording clarifies that source checks were AI-assisted, specialist review is pending, and this is a working draft. No independent scholarly review or original human study is represented as complete.

The audio player streams the published files through a host with byte-range support so listeners can seek within a chapter. Direct downloads remain available on the website.
