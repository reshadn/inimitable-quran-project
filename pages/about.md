# The Inimitable Qur’an Project

## Read closely. Examine the evidence.

The Qur’an presents itself as revelation and challenges its doubters to produce a comparable surah. This project asks what its language accomplishes, what a fair comparison would require, and how far the evidence supports its claim to revelation.

You do not have to accept that conclusion to take part. We are developing an affirmative, evidence-led case that welcomes serious criticism. Believing readers, skeptical readers, Arabic specialists, historians, translators, statisticians, and curious newcomers should all be able to inspect the argument.

## A book being written in public

*The Qur’an Examined* begins with a phrase and a complete short surah. In Qur’an 19:4, the head “has blazed with gray hair.” What does making the head the grammatical subject contribute to the image and the prayer? In al-ʿAṣr, how do the oath, warning, exception, and mutual responsibilities form a complete composition?

The approach keeps four questions separate: What is present in the text? What does it accomplish? How well does it compare with strong alternatives? What does that tell us about origin?

Distinctiveness does not automatically establish excellence. Excellence does not automatically establish revelation. The book must explain those steps, including the possibility of exceptional human authorship.

## What is available now

The expanded research edition, dated 20 September 2026, contains approximately 14,600 words across an opening essay and six chapters, with sources and working translations. Four new chapters examine the challenge passages, the distance between the central claims, selected classical theories, and responsible statistical analysis. It is a working draft, not a completed proof or finished book.

The source catalog contains 71 bibliography records for candidate works and resources:

- 16 bounded passage or documentation inspections.
- 29 catalog or abstract confirmations.
- 26 discovery leads awaiting verification.

No complete book or article is claimed as read in the recorded research passes. A confirmed title is not a verified argument, and an author's inclusion does not imply endorsement of this project's conclusion.

A reproducible computational pilot describes the selected text and its counting rules. It does not measure divinity or establish literary superiority. Independent Arabic review, stronger comparisons, fuller source reading, and original human studies remain ahead. The proposed expert, reader, and recitation studies have not been run.

The manuscript drafts and source checks were produced with AI assistance. They have not received independent human scholarly review.

## Open research and community review

The project invites corrections, contrary evidence, better comparisons, and reproducible analyses through [the public repository](https://github.com/reshadn/inimitable-quran-project). Community review helps improve the work. It is not a substitute for independent academic validation, and publication here does not mean a claim has received it.

Original project prose is released under CC BY 4.0 and project code under MIT. Third-party texts, translations, recordings, and datasets retain their own terms. The repository’s license files identify the applicable permissions.

Read the draft, follow a citation, or challenge one specific claim. A useful contribution leaves the next reader with a clearer argument and better evidence.
