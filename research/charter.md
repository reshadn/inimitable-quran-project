# Research charter

Version 0.1 · 16 September 2026 · Working research program

## Purpose

Write an affirmative, evidence-led case for the Qur’an’s inimitability that readers can examine without first accepting revelation. The project begins with the text, develops defensible comparisons, and then considers the inference to origin. The theological claim is stronger than literary distinctiveness or superiority. The books must explain that distance rather than hide it.

The agreed program comprises an accessible flagship, an academic monograph, a short illustrated edition, and a reproducible research companion. These are planned deliverables. The present package is an initial dossier, sample manuscript, and descriptive computational pilot. Human studies have not begun, experts have not been retained, and no experimental result is asserted.

## Evidence levels

| Level | Question | Appropriate evidence | Does not establish by itself |
|---|---|---|---|
| Textual observation | What occurs in the passage? | Verified reading, morphological and syntactic analysis | Literary excellence |
| Functional interpretation | What does the choice accomplish here? | Context, alternative readings, careful comparisons | Universal agreement |
| Comparative evaluation | How does this achievement compare? | Strong controls, explicit criteria, competent readers | Human impossibility |
| Historical inference | What composition and transmission circumstances are supported? | Dated evidence, source criticism, manuscript and historical scholarship | A theological conclusion |
| Philosophical inference | Which explanation best accounts for the combined evidence? | Explicit premises and competing explanations, including exceptional human authorship | A mechanically computed probability of revelation |

## Editorial standard

Each case study presents the Arabic, an original or attributed translation, a small number of consequential features, their function in context, plausible disagreement, and the limit of the conclusion. Arabic alternatives are always labeled as analytical examples, never scripture. A deliberately plain paraphrase teaches a distinction; it is not a strong literary opponent.

Classical authors will be allowed to disagree. Their theological schools and purposes will be identified where relevant. Modern literary scholars will not be presented as endorsing inimitability simply because they recognize literary qualities. A reported event will not become verified history merely through repetition in later books.

The reader should be able to distinguish a passage inspected in its source from a title discovered in a bibliography. A page number from a digital transcription is not a verified print-edition page. Restricted or inaccessible sources remain explicit access gaps.

## Boundaries

Language leads the investigation. Composition, cognition, recitation, translation, reception, historical circumstances, and transmission supply additional lines of inquiry. Numerical patterns and scientific-miracle claims are separate research questions. They receive no evidential credit until their definitions, sampling choices, historical interpretation, and alternatives have been tested.

No findings will be described as proof that every possible human composition fails. A finite comparison has a finite scope. Conversely, uncertainty will not be used as a reason to avoid assessing the affirmative case. The final conclusion should state both what the evidence supports and which philosophical premises are required.

## Roles and near-term sequence

The author is accountable for the argument and writing; Arabic specialists for independent language review; a research editor for source verification; a statistician for study design; and independent reviewers for replication and criticism. These are unfilled proposed roles, not a hired team.

1. Finish source-level review of the two sample chapters and a short opening essay.
2. Acquire and examine the foundational sources in the dossier, including contrary scholarship.
3. Develop full case studies of the remaining ten editorial surahs.
4. Assemble strong comparison texts with dated provenance and documented selection rules.
5. Pilot rubrics with consenting qualified readers; revise ambiguous measures.
6. Lock protocols, budgets, sample-size justifications, and data-management procedures before confirmatory recruitment.

The next internal milestone is a reviewer-ready sample package and source audit. External recruitment, payment, registration, or publication requires a separately authorized scope. Local research and drafting can continue without those actions.

## Completion standard

The complete program needs verified source locations, qualified Arabic review, reproducible measurements, original studies, contrary-result reporting, historical source criticism, and independent review. Present completion is limited to the files and checks listed in the package’s status report.
