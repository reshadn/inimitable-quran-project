# Research edition 0.3

30 September 2026. Public working draft with an opening essay and eight chapters, approximately 19,000 whitespace-delimited words including notes and markup. The full book remains in development. Independent human scholarly review is pending.

## New in this edition

**Economy and Implication** examines 2:179 in relation to its preceding verse, and the recoverable object in 93:3. It distinguishes expressed relations, contextual reconstruction, historical commentary, and unsupported extensions. Its original teaching paraphrases are explicitly not competitive literary specimens or Qur’anic text.

**Sound and Meaning** reads all eleven verses of al-Ḍuḥā. It compares an editorial discourse partition with approximate verse-end sound labels, including places where semantic changes occur without a new sound label. It separates text, reading convention, performance, and listener response.

The two new chapters are currently **text-only**. The seven existing English narrations remain available, totaling approximately 106 minutes. No new recording or human listening study accompanies this edition.

## What the computation establishes

[The close-reading inventory](../analysis/close-reading-observations.md) uses the unchanged, hash-verified Tanzil corpus and the pre-existing written-token rule. It counts 8 tokens in 2:179, 5 in 93:3, and 40 across surah 93. Approximate pausal endings and discourse functions are manual editorial annotations that require independent review. Counting those labels does not validate their phonetic or interpretive accuracy.

No acoustic data were collected. No reader study, significance test, probability of divine authorship, or comparison establishing superiority was conducted. The corpus manifest remains unchanged.
