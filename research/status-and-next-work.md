## Material corrections made during review

The opening now distinguishes literary distinctiveness, exceptional achievement, universal human incapacity, and revelation as separate propositions. The methods describe crossed reader/passage observations and versions nested within passages. Comparative claims require a contrast with human controls, and equivalence requires a justified margin. The al-ʿAṣr chapter does not infer equal authority from mutual counsel.
