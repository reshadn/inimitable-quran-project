Literary explanations are kept separate from grammatical observations and any proposed reader effects.

## What the computation establishes

[The address inventory](../analysis/address-map.md) extracts 18 selected reference-bearing anchors from the unchanged, hash-verified Tanzil corpus. Manual labels identify a grammatical component, referent, person, speech layer, and proposed discourse role. A declared rule evaluates two selected transition pairs and two exclusion controls. Under the supplied labels, the illustrative transitions meet the rule in directions 3→2 and 2→3; the controls do not.

These are transparent consequences of selected annotations. The program does not validate those assignments, discover all instances of iltifāt, establish a global frequency, or demonstrate a reader effect. There is no significance test, novelty claim, literary superiority score, or probability of revelation.
